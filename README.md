# NAILED & Lashed by Mona — Digital Studio Case Study

![NAILED & Lashed by Mona](assets/hero.webp)

A public portfolio case study by [SolarSpin Technologies](https://freeman-ipumbu.pages.dev/) for the installable booking, training-enquiry, loyalty and client-operations platform created for **NAILED & Lashed by Mona**, a nail and lash business in Windhoek, Namibia.

## The result

The project turns a visual beauty brand into a complete appointment and operations system:

- immersive, responsive client-work lookbook;
- four-step booking flow with studio visits and priced house-call zones;
- configurable service prices, booking fees and work days;
- cash, eWallet and bank-transfer payment tracking;
- a separate individual-training journey for beginner gel, advanced nail art and lash-training enquiries;
- consented training leads with a dedicated reference, preferred contact channel, status pipeline and private CSV export;
- Mona Luxe Circle client accounts with tier progress, reward redemption, referrals and faster repeat booking;
- a private client CRM with booking, payment, loyalty and retention visibility;
- an enhanced identity plus loyalty and training rollout kits for Instagram, Stories and WhatsApp;
- protected owner workspace for bookings, training leads, clients and business controls;
- installable PWA, offline fallback and motion-reduction support;
- bounded request bodies, same-origin mutation checks, rate limiting and owner authentication;
- deployment on Cloudflare Pages with KV-backed persistence.

## Experience principles

1. **Real work first.** Client photography carries the interface instead of decorative filler.
2. **No mystery costs.** Services, booking fees and house-call travel charges stay visible throughout the flow.
3. **Private where it matters.** The exact studio address and payment destination are disclosed only after confirmation.
4. **Owner-friendly operations.** Mona can change pricing, schedule and payment state without editing code.
5. **Mobile is the main stage.** Thumb-friendly controls, persistent booking access and carefully managed safe areas make the journey natural on a phone.

## Training journey

Training is intentionally separated from appointment booking. A student chooses a published course path, selects WhatsApp, phone or email as the preferred reply channel, accepts the enquiry privacy notice and receives a dedicated reference. Mona then confirms availability, format, location and any payment step directly.

Launch offers documented in the experience:

- **Beginner Gel Nail Training with complete kit — N$1,500:** nail anatomy, natural-nail preparation, nail art and design, filing technique, complete gel kit and certificate of course completion.
- **Beginner Gel Nail Training without kit — N$900:** the same beginner curriculum and certificate, without the take-home kit.
- **Advanced Nail Art Training — N$500:** 3D art, ombré, drawings or hand-painted detail, and animal-print effects.
- **Lash training — on request:** format and quotation are confirmed directly by Mona.

Training is individual and arranged on request. The public site does not promise a date, duration, venue, capacity or enrolment before Mona confirms it. Training enquiries remain separate from Circle accounts and do not automatically earn loyalty rewards.

StudioDesk gives Mona a private training pipeline—new, contacted, scheduled, deposit pending, enrolled, completed or cancelled—plus offer configuration, filtering, status counts and spreadsheet-safe export. These are operational workflow states, not fabricated enrolment, revenue or completion results.

## Mona Luxe Circle launch rules

- **Earn:** 8 Gloss Points for every N$1 of eligible service spend, plus 300 welcome points when a client joins.
- **Refer:** 1,000 points for the referrer and 500 points for the friend after the friend completes and pays for an eligible first appointment.
- **Progress:** Glow begins at 0 lifetime points, Luxe at 5,000 and Icon at 15,000.
- **Redeem:** four launch rewards unlock at 2,500, 5,000, 9,000 and 14,000 available points.

Travel fees do not earn points. Rewards have no cash value and remain subject to the published Mona Luxe Circle terms.
