The brief

Luxury energy.
Zero booking friction.

Mona needed more than a brochure. The business serves clients from Khomasdal and through house calls, accepts multiple payment methods, teaches individual nail sessions and grows through repeat relationships and visual trust. The experience had to make all of that feel effortless on the device clients actually use.

We built connected paths: explore real work, understand transparent starting prices, enquire about training, join Mona Luxe Circle, choose a studio visit or mobile route, request a slot, return faster, and let Mona run the business from a protected workspace.

Booking integrity · live in production

A request that
understands time.

Availability is calculated across a rolling 90-day window for the selected service and appointment mode. Clients see which dates are open, fully booked, closed or out of valid times before they choose a slot; past dates and travel-adjusted departure times are rejected in the interface and checked again by the server.

OpenSlots shown

Only times that fit the service, cleanup and route remain selectable.

Fully bookedDemand, honestly

Valid intervals exist, but every one is already held by another request.

No slots leftTime, honestly

The studio is open, but the remaining starts have passed or no longer fit.

ClosedSchedule respected

Mona’s configured work days shape the calendar without manual client follow-up.

The state cards explain the production behaviour; they are not a fabricated snapshot of Mona’s current calendar.

Built to work

Not a mockup.
A connected system.

Cloudflare Pages and KV persistence support bookings, training enquiries, client accounts, rewards and live configuration. A private SQLite-backed Durable Object owns appointment interval claims; public requests remain bounded and rate-limited, sessions stay opaque, and no banking credentials are stored.

Integrity boundary: the lock service prevents concurrent overlap, while KV remains Mona’s operational studio record and her confirmation remains the final service commitment.
